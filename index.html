About Our School

Building Future Leaders Since 1926

Polegoda Maha Vidyalaya has proudly served generations of students with dedication, discipline, and academic excellence. Our mission is to nurture students with knowledge, creativity, leadership, and strong moral values.

With modern education facilities, experienced teachers, and a vibrant student community, we continue to shape future leaders prepared for the modern world.
